All recovery-related assistance is approached through legitimate legal, investigative, and reporting procedures. We do not encourage unlawful access, retaliation, unauthorized account access, or attempts to reverse blockchain transactions through improper means.

Scam & Fraud Awareness

Understanding common tactics can help individuals recognize warning signs and avoid further losses.

Phishing

Fake websites, messages, emails, or links may be designed to obtain sensitive information or gain unauthorized access to accounts.

Fake Investment Platforms

Fraudulent platforms may display fabricated balances, profits, or withdrawal requirements to persuade victims to send additional funds.

Impersonation

Scammers may impersonate companies, professionals, customer-support representatives, or other trusted individuals.

Romance & Social Engineering Scams

Criminals may build trust online before introducing financial requests, investment opportunities, or cryptocurrency transfers.

Protect Your Remaining Digital Assets

If you believe you have been targeted by a cryptocurrency scam, protecting remaining assets should be a priority.

Never share your recovery phrase or private key. Legitimate investigators and advisors should not need credentials that provide direct control of your assets.
Be cautious of recovery scams. Fraudsters sometimes target previous victims by promising guaranteed recovery in exchange for additional payments or sensitive information.
Preserve evidence. Keep transaction IDs, wallet addresses, communications, screenshots, dates, platform information, and other relevant records.
Do not send additional funds simply because someone promises recovery. Verify the identity, credentials, and legitimacy of anyone offering professional assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information about our approach to digital-asset fraud cases.

Can TrustGuard Blockchain Intelligence guarantee recovery?

No. Recovery cannot be guaranteed. Our role is to help clients understand available evidence, blockchain activity, security concerns, legitimate reporting channels, and possible investigative or legal avenues.

Do you need my private key or wallet recovery phrase?

No. Never provide your private key, seed phrase, recovery phrase, password, or authentication code to someone claiming to investigate or recover your cryptocurrency.

What information may be useful for an assessment?

Depending on the situation, useful information may include transaction IDs, wallet addresses, relevant communications, screenshots, dates, platform information, and other records connected to the incident.

Can blockchain analysis identify a person?

Blockchain data can provide information about transactions and addresses, but an address does not automatically reveal the identity of the person controlling it. Additional evidence or professional investigation may be required.

What happens after an assessment?

Depending on the available information, clients may receive an explanation of relevant findings and possible reporting, investigative, security, legal, or professional referral options.

Are recovery-related procedures always possible?

No. The available options depend on the facts of the case, available evidence, transaction history, jurisdiction, the parties involved, and the actions available to relevant professionals, platforms, or authorities.
