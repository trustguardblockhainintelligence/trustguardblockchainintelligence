Possible Recovery Through Legitimate Legal & Reporting Procedures

Understanding your available options can be an important first step after a cryptocurrency loss.

Losing cryptocurrency does not necessarily mean every legitimate avenue has been exhausted. Depending on the circumstances, available evidence, transaction history, jurisdiction, and the parties involved, victims may have investigative, reporting, civil, or other lawful avenues that could potentially assist with pursuing recovery.

TrustGuard Blockchain Intelligence helps clients organize relevant information, understand blockchain activity, document evidence, identify appropriate reporting channels, and, where appropriate, coordinate referrals to qualified lawyers, cybersecurity professionals, blockchain-forensics specialists, cryptocurrency exchanges, or relevant authorities.

Recovery-related assistance is approached strictly through legitimate legal, investigative, and reporting procedures. We do not encourage unauthorized access, retaliation, unlawful activity, or attempts to reverse blockchain transactions through improper means.

Important: We cannot guarantee the recovery of lost cryptocurrency or promise a particular outcome. Each case depends on its individual evidence, circumstances, jurisdiction, and the actions available to relevant professionals, platforms, or authorities.

Scam & Fraud Awareness

Recognizing common tactics can help individuals avoid further losses.

Phishing

Fake websites, messages, emails, or links may be designed to obtain sensitive information or unauthorized account access.

Fake Investment Platforms

Fraudulent platforms may display fabricated balances or profits and later demand additional payments before allowing withdrawals.

Impersonation

Scammers may impersonate companies, professionals, customer-support representatives, investigators, or other trusted individuals.

Romance & Social Engineering

Criminals may build relationships or trust online before introducing financial requests, investment opportunities, or cryptocurrency transfers.

Protect Your Remaining Digital Assets

If you believe you have been targeted by a cryptocurrency scam, protecting remaining assets should be a priority.

Never Share Private Keys

Legitimate investigators or advisors should not require your private key, seed phrase, or wallet recovery phrase.

Beware of Recovery Scams

Previous victims are sometimes targeted again by people promising guaranteed recovery in exchange for additional money or sensitive information.

Preserve Your Evidence

Keep transaction IDs, wallet addresses, communications, screenshots, dates, platform information, and other relevant records.

Verify Professional Assistance

Be cautious of anyone demanding payment while promising a guaranteed result. Verify identities, credentials, and legitimate professional channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our approach to cryptocurrency-related fraud cases.

Can TrustGuard guarantee cryptocurrency recovery?

No. We do not guarantee recovery or promise a particular outcome. We help clients understand available evidence and possible legitimate reporting, investigative, security, or legal avenues.

Do you need my private key or recovery phrase?

No. Never provide your private key, seed phrase, wallet recovery phrase, password, or authentication code to someone claiming to investigate or recover cryptocurrency.

What information may be useful for an assessment?

Depending on the circumstances, useful information may include transaction IDs, wallet addresses, relevant communications, screenshots, dates, platform information, and other records connected to the incident.

Can a blockchain address identify a person?

Not automatically. Blockchain data can provide information about transactions and addresses, but identifying a person may require additional evidence or professional investigation.

What happens after an assessment?

Depending on the available information, clients may receive an explanation of relevant findings and possible reporting, investigative, security, legal, or professional referral options.

Are recovery-related avenues always available?

No. Available options depend on the facts, evidence, transaction history, jurisdiction, parties involved, and the actions available to relevant professionals, platforms, or authorities.
