About TrustGuard Blockchain Intelligence

Evidence-based support for people and businesses dealing with cryptocurrency-related fraud.

TrustGuard Blockchain Intelligence is a digital-asset fraud analysis and victim-support organization focused on helping individuals and businesses better understand cryptocurrency-related fraud.

We provide blockchain transaction analysis, evidence documentation, fraud-awareness education, wallet-security guidance, and advisory support for individuals who have experienced suspected cryptocurrency scams or unauthorized transactions.

Our approach is evidence-based and transparent. We examine available information, explain what can and cannot be established from blockchain data, and help clients identify appropriate reporting, investigative, or professional avenues.

Our mission is simple: to replace confusion with clarity, protect victims from further losses, and promote responsible approaches to digital-asset fraud investigation and possible recovery.

Fraud Awareness

Understanding common tactics can help individuals recognize warning signs before further losses occur.

Phishing

Fraudsters may use fake websites, messages, emails, or links designed to obtain sensitive information or gain access to accounts.

Fake Investment Platforms

Fraudulent platforms may display fabricated balances, profits, or withdrawal requirements to persuade victims to send additional funds.

Impersonation

Scammers may impersonate companies, professionals, customer-support representatives, or other trusted individuals.

Romance & Social Engineering Scams

Criminals may build relationships or trust online before introducing financial requests, investment opportunities, or cryptocurrency transfers.

Important Notice

TrustGuard Blockchain Intelligence does not guarantee the recovery of lost cryptocurrency or promise that funds can be returned.

Blockchain transactions are generally irreversible. Blockchain analysis may provide useful information about transactions and wallet activity, but it does not by itself guarantee identification of the person controlling an address or recovery of funds.

We do not request private keys, wallet recovery phrases, passwords, authentication codes, or other credentials that could provide access to digital assets.

Where appropriate, clients may be directed toward qualified lawyers, cybersecurity professionals, blockchain-forensics specialists, cryptocurrency exchanges, or relevant authorities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to some common questions about our approach.

Can TrustGuard Blockchain Intelligence guarantee recovery of cryptocurrency?

No. Cryptocurrency recovery cannot be guaranteed. Our role is to help clients understand available evidence, blockchain activity, security concerns, and legitimate next steps.

Do you need my private key or wallet recovery phrase?

No. Never provide your private key, seed phrase, recovery phrase, password, or authentication code to someone claiming to investigate or recover your cryptocurrency.

What information may be useful for an assessment?

Depending on the situation, useful information may include transaction IDs, wallet addresses, relevant communications, screenshots, dates, platform information, and other records connected to the incident.

Can blockchain analysis identify a person?

Blockchain data can provide information about transactions and addresses, but an address does not automatically reveal the identity of the person controlling it. Additional evidence or professional investigation may be required.

What happens after an assessment?

Depending on the available information, clients may receive an explanation of relevant findings and possible reporting, investigative, security, or professional referral options.
